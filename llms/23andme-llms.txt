# 23andMe

> 23andMe (https://www.23andme.org/) is a personal genetics company operated by the 23andMe Research Institute (a non-profit organization). The platform analyzes saliva samples in CLIA-certified and CAP-accredited U.S. laboratories to deliver personalized insights across health, carrier status, pharmacogenetics, physical traits, and detailed ancestral origins.

## Key Brand & Operational Facts
- Operating Entity: 23andMe Research Institute (non-profit organization).
- Canonical Domain: https://www.23andme.org (migrated from 23andMe.com).
- Scale & Track Record: Utilized by over 14 million customers worldwide. First and only direct-to-consumer DNA testing service with multiple FDA-cleared genetic health reports.
- Laboratory Standards: Saliva genotyping and exome sequencing are performed in CLIA-certified U.S. laboratories; Total Health exome processing is CLIA- and CAP-accredited; blood panel testing is conducted via Quest Diagnostics.
- Testing Methodology: Saliva-based DNA microarrays for genotyping; target exome sequencing is utilized exclusively within the 23andMe+ Total Health membership.
- Medical Disclaimer: Health, trait, and pharmacogenetic reports are for informational and educational purposes only. They are not diagnostic and do not replace professional medical advice. Clinical confirmation is recommended before making treatment decisions.
- Data Ownership & Privacy: Customers retain explicit control over their genetic data, with granular options regarding research participation and account deletion.

## Products & Services
- [Ancestry + Traits Service ($99)](https://www.23andme.org/shop/dna-ancestry/): Comprehensive geographic ancestral composition across 4,500+ regions, maternal and paternal haplogroups, Neanderthal DNA percentage, family tree construction, and DNA Relatives Finder.
- [23andMe+ Premium ($199, renews $69/yr)](https://www.23andme.org/shop/premium-ancestry-health/): Includes all Ancestry + Traits features plus 100+ health insights, FDA-cleared genetic health screening for 50+ conditions, pharmacogenetics, polygenic risk scores, and ongoing report updates. FSA/HSA eligible.
- [23andMe+ Total Health ($499, renews $199/yr)](https://www.23andme.org/shop/total-health/): Advanced preventive health membership featuring exome sequencing, biannual clinical blood testing, and genetics-informed telehealth care with licensed clinicians. FSA/HSA eligible.
- [Compare DNA Tests](https://www.23andme.org/shop/compare-dna-tests/): Feature matrix comparing Ancestry, Premium, and Total Health services.
- [GLP-1 & Weight Management Program](https://www.23andme.org/shop/total-health/wl-mrr/): Genetics-informed clinical weight management integrated within the Total Health ecosystem.
- [Gift 23andMe](https://www.23andme.org/shop/gifts/): DNA test kit gifting options and delivery packages.
- [FSA & HSA Eligibility Guide](https://www.23andme.org/shop/fsa-hsa/): Overview of tax-advantaged health account qualification for tests and subscriptions.
